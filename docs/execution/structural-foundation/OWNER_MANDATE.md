Act as Builderad's Principal Software Architect and Executive Engineering Supervisor.

**Builderad must be engineered as one coherent, extensible system, with known structural foundations, responsibility ownership, dependency relationships, and a continuous construction program.**

3. Reliable foundations.
4. Safe dependency direction.
5. Accumulation of specialized expertise.
6. Controlled architectural evolution.
7. Verifiable implementation.
8. Long-term maintainability.
9. Efficient construction with minimal unnecessary work.

The Owner does require **necessary shared foundations to become real, tested code when their responsibilities and dependencies justify implementation**.

This mandate must produce a continuous, executable engineering construction program, not another theoretical architecture proposal.

### Governing principle

**Establish the structural responsibilities of the known system, preserve what is already correct, implement what is actually necessary, and extend the system through bounded, independently verifiable changes.**

An architecture should not promise that its core will never change.

It must make changes understandable, appropriately localized, compatible where required, and testable.

## 7. ESTABLISH HOW EXPERTISE ACCUMULATES

Builderad must be able to continuously turn qualified research and expert knowledge into better operational capabilities.

The engineering process is:

**Research → Qualification → Mechanism Understanding → Classification → Domain Ownership → Implementation or Knowledge Update → Verification → Evaluation → Evolution**

A research finding may become:

- A qualified evidence source.
- A new observation meaning.
- A constraint.
- An applicability policy.
- A platform-specific interpretation.
- A shared analytical calculation.
- A specialist diagnosis.
- An experiment strategy.
- A decision-domain improvement.
- An authorized action capability.
- A correction to an existing method.
- Qualified reference knowledge requiring no new code.

Do not assume every discovery becomes a class, method, database table, or registered capability.

### Platform specialization

Before implementing platform-dependent expertise, establish a bounded Platform Mechanism & Operations study.

Distinguish:

- Officially documented mechanisms.
- Directly observed behavior.
- Established expert practice.
- Testable hypotheses.
- Unknown or inaccessible mechanisms.

Inspect, as applicable:

- Native object and product semantics.
- Optimization goals and delivery behavior.
- Available observation surfaces.
- Signal quality and measurement.
- Attribution limitations.
- API read and write operations.
- Permissions and access conditions.
- Geographic and account restrictions.
- Version and policy changes.
- Economic interpretation.
- Competitor and professional approaches.
- Testable counterexamples.

Do not claim privileged knowledge of proprietary advertising algorithms.

### Shared versus specialized intelligence

An analytical mechanism may be reused across platforms only when its meanings, assumptions, evidence requirements, and output interpretation remain valid.

Do not assume:

`Platform × Sector × Market = Independent Engine`

Do not force Google Search Terms, Meta delivery semantics, and general market-demand measurements into one false universal measurement model.

Reuse shared intelligence only where semantic equivalence or a qualified transformation is established.

### Evolution

Determine how an existing capability can be:

- Extended.
- Corrected.
- Replaced.
- Versioned.
- Retired.
- Requalified after evidence withdrawal.

Historical decisions must remain interpretable under the versions and evidence available when they were made.

No promise of zero future core changes is allowed.

The goal is controlled change impact, not an immutable architecture.

## 9. ARCHITECTURAL STRESS AND CHANGE-IMPACT PROOF

The architecture must be evaluated against materially different scenarios.

At minimum:

**S1 — Meta versus Google**

Two platform specializations with different native optimization, delivery, measurement, and bidding semantics.

**S2 — Source-independent intelligence**

A market or commercial analysis that does not require an advertising account or declared goal.

**S3 — Changing goals**

The same historical qualified evidence is evaluated against a changed Current Intent without rewriting source truth.

**S4 — Changing markets or activity**

A Business in one country targeting several other markets; different commercial outcomes across real estate, e-commerce, and services.

**S5 — Evidence correction**

A late correction changes a derived assessment while preserving evidence lineage and decision history.

**S6 — Source-right withdrawal**

Consumption and downstream reliance respect newly invalid use rights.

**S7 — Method failure or replacement**

A new specialist version does not silently reinterpret historical results, and failure does not become a supported conclusion.

**S8 — Conflicting assessments**

Platform, market, and commercial analyses disagree; the system does not invent certainty or arbitrary precedence.

**S9 — Unauthorized action**

A recommendation cannot automatically mutate an advertising account.

**S10 — Uncertain external effect**

A future execution result may be unknown; the architecture must not prescribe blind replay.

**S11 — Verification and causality**

Observed performance changes are not automatically accepted as causal incremental impact.

**S12 — Agent consumption**

A future AI agent cannot bypass normal evidence rights, decision boundaries, or execution authorization.

For each scenario record:

- Existing responsibilities involved.
- Assumption being challenged.
- Expected valid behavior.
- Current supporting code or contract.
- Current proof level.
- Actual architectural gap, if any.
- Change-impact boundary.
- Required future tests.

Use these proof categories:

`PROVEN_IN_EXECUTABLE_CODE`

`DESIGN_VALIDATED_WITH_LIMITS`

`FUTURE_IMPLEMENTATION_OR_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED`

These are evidence descriptions, not replacement Project Control Model lifecycle states.

Do not report a future behavior as passing executable tests.

When an actual foundation package is implemented, replace relevant design-only claims with real behavioral and negative tests.

Avoid test doubles that prove only that a class can return a constant response.
