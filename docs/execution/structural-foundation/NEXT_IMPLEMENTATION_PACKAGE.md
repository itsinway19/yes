## 3. Smallest real behavior and source truth

**User job / consumer:** an authorized Business operator records and reviews a dated inquiry-cohort evidence declaration for their own Business, can correct it with preserved history, and can see what is asserted, missing, expired or not qualified for analysis. This evidence readback is the package's actual consumer; a future Case A diagnostic is a separate consumer. No requirement for a CRM, campaign, connected provider or advertiser goal.

Initial supply route proposed for this package: an explicitly authorized Business-side human declaration, with source identity, declared recording basis and supporting references where available. It stores a **source assertion**, not a verified inquiry, sale, customer identity, provider observation or profit. It does not give the actor authority to certify a third-party system's records. A future lawful source adapter can supply its own evidence kind through the same semantic boundary only after its own qualification; manual entry is not a permanent architecture requirement.

The readback must always state the source/assurance kind: «بيانات صرّح بها النشاط؛ لم تتحقق Builderad من الوقائع الأصلية». Saving or passing structural checks must not remove that limit. An asserted subset/criterion remains asserted. A future analytical reader must assess sufficiency for its specific claim; it cannot trust an `eligible=true`, `verified=true` or display-permission field supplied by the browser.

Payload meaning is limited to: Business-scoped source reference; stable cohort definition and revision; interval/timezone/as-of; cost with currency/precision/component/allocation basis when provided; inquiry count when provided; accepted count plus criterion/ref/version/cutoff/maturity when provided; counting/linkage/coverage declaration; input/supporting references and correction reason. Missing axes stay missing. No PII, messages, transcripts, customer directory, identity matching engine, generic file ingestion, sales/settlement facts, automatic FX or arbitrary extensible JSON truth store in this package.

A correction appends a new admitted revision linked to its predecessor. A stale correction conflicts; it must not adopt a newer revision while retaining an old form. Record/readback distinguishes current applicability from retained history. A lost save response uses the same command identity/body; changed intent uses a new command. Fresh readback still requalifies current authority.
