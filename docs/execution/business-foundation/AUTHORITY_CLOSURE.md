## Binding transition policy

Previously issued, active Workspace-qualified Business rights retain their original identity, realm, qualification, membership/incarnation, relationship, expiry, lifecycle and revocation requirements. Their already-authorized reads/edits and legitimate withdrawal/ending remain possible. Business choices must not silently conceal those qualified paths. No row is converted to a direct grant, reactivated or deleted. A retained record alone never establishes current authority.

Every **new** Business grant/qualification must originate from an independent Business path. An old Workspace relationship, membership or ancestor cannot issue a new custodian, staff child or operating mandate. Exact historical receipt recovery still requires current qualified sender authority and matching command identity/payload; returning it creates nothing and does not revive the referenced grant.

Future file continuity uses explicit Business appointment through the existing internal-file custody command and qualification graph: `internal_client_file_admin_v1` version2, direct realm, exact Business target and five current-declaration actions only. This is an independent, expressly appointed file right, not general Business ownership, automatic employee promotion, external representation, private-history access or Source/data permission. Its issuer is an existing independent creator or an explicitly qualified direct file custodian. A historical Workspace custodian cannot convert its own authority into this new role. New direct custody is revocable through its qualification and descendants remain dependent on that exact qualification.

The original creator may explicitly appoint a backup before departure; that independent backup continues the same bounded file work. Workspace ownership takeover grants nothing. Existing legitimate version1 custody remains unchanged and does not acquire new issuance powers.
