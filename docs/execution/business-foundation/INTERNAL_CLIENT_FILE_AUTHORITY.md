## Historical proposal — superseded by the Owner adoption above

Approve a versioned internal-file custody policy using the existing qualification receipts, grant chains, Business identity and Workspace team context. Its basis is creating and explicitly administering an internal record, not representing the external establishment.

Requested policy boundary:

1. The original creator may explicitly enroll a newly created internal file for team administration in a Workspace they are currently qualified to administer. This is opt-in; no automatic enrollment of historical files or all Workspace activities.
2. Enrollment qualifies only record read, record-name/field management, declared markets, declared intent and declared preferences. Delegation is explicit per Business and action to current team members, initially without onward delegation. It must remain bounded to the chosen organizational realm, with independent sibling revocation and current membership checks.
3. Its organizational relationship must retain provenance identifying internal-file custody. It must not be represented as proof that an external client delegated its establishment. Validate whether the current relationship basis can express this without widening external-agency semantics before implementing it.
4. No source/connection grant, client-boundary management, data-use authority, material publication/export, external-provider operation, legal-ownership claim or external-client qualification follows. Those need their independent accepted contracts. Existing private creator-only inquiry custody must not become staff-readable implicitly.
5. Revocation or team departure denies affected future use and requalifies operations under the existing concurrency contract; independent paths and retained lawful history survive. A client registering later or another agency creating a similar file acquires no access or identity merge.

This is a new issuer policy decision, even if implemented using the existing `qualified_policy` representation. It does not require a new Organization entity or a second permission engine. The exact permission enforcement and any additive migration must follow the accepted decision; neither a blanket draft promotion nor a production synthetic root is authorized by this record.
