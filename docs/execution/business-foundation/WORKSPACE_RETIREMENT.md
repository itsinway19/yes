## Integration interfaces

- Business selection: `{realm_kind:"direct",requirements:[{requirement_kind,leaf_grant_id}]}`. Business-to-Business agency is carried by existing qualified agency/employee receipts; no Workspace realm or substitute membership system.
- Customers: `kind=users|businesses`; Business states declared_draft/active/suspended/archived; previous state comes from trusted suspension event, not client input.
- Service approval: `/businesses/{business}/services[/{service}]`; exact command_id/service/purpose/expected_revision/business_revision/authority_selection. Business service approval is independent of context.manage, lifecycle.manage, creator identity, staff and agency qualification.

- Commercial: Business beneficiary identity replaces Workspace targeting; configuration/catalog/market authority does not become client content authority or subscription issuance.
