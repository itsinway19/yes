## 3. Step A — derive evidence from the N06 question

The accepted N06 question asks where relevant advertising effort should be directed and away from which unsuitable opportunities. A substantive answer needs **a relation between an established business purpose and a real directed-effort condition**, not an isolated declaration or metric. The cheapest candidate proposition is:

**For this exact object's established purpose of reaching customers from a declared country, the source-qualified configuration contains a material eligibility obstruction; that obstruction needs review/correction to serve that purpose.**

This is a candidate class, not a discovered fact, approved intervention or permanent Targeting model. Configuration consistency is selected because §17 of the accepted design permits it without performance proof, and the other candidates below require additional unestablished outcome/customer/market relationships. No provider fields were used to define the requirement. Steps A and B preceded official provider research.

Two semantic safeguards are essential:

1. **DTM is a desired customer-origin set, not an exclusive delivery whitelist.** An object including another country is not automatically wrong. A subset can be legitimate specialization; not every campaign must cover every DTM country. Priority, an exclusive market restriction or campaign purpose cannot be inferred from DTM ordering, business location, sector or country difference.
2. **Provider geo eligibility is not customer origin, nationality, residence, delivery or accepted outcome.** The case must justify the relationship between its declared customer-country purpose and the source's actual geo membership rule. Interest-based eligibility, visitors, expansion, inheritance or another intentional route can invalidate a simplistic mismatch. If that relationship is unknown, withhold the customer-scope obstruction claim even if the configured setting can be described.

Thus a different-country screenshot alone is insufficient. A complete source-qualified exclusion or restrictive eligible scope, an object-purpose premise with established accepted semantic standing (meaning, scope, provenance, authority and input contract), and a justified geography/customer relation can support a bounded obstruction conclusion. A real case cannot silently establish the missing purpose contract; §4.1 records its current gap. It still proves no missed sale, waste, poorer performance or universal corrective action. A preservation conclusion similarly needs positive question-relevant support; absence of a detected obstruction is not proof the entire targeting should remain unchanged.
