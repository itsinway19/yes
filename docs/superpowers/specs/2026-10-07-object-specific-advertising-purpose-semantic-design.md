## 5. Semantic meaning

**Object-specific advertising purpose is the authorized, explicitly confirmed declaration of what identified advertising effort is being used to accomplish for this business, within its declared applicability and business-effective time.** It is an intent-like **declaration fact serving as a scoped business decision premise**. Canonical acceptance establishes what the business declared, not effectiveness, actual behavior, opportunity, achievement or permission to act.

The minimum confirmed meaning contains the business use being asserted and the exact relation that makes it relevant to the consuming question. For example: “use this object to acquire property inquiries from customers originating in Saudi Arabia.” The confirmed country relation means intended customer origin; it does not mean provider geo eligibility, residence, nationality, an exclusive delivery whitelist or observed buyers. “Saudi campaign” alone is insufficient.

The readable explanation may express the broader use, but a consumer must receive the explicit confirmed premise it relies on, with its meaning and limits. It may not extract a new canonical country restriction, audience condition or goal from prose. If a material term such as “qualified inquiry” is decisive, its acceptance meaning must be legitimately established under the distinct outcome-meaning responsibility; this contract neither invents it nor requires an outcome catalog for every purpose. A country eligibility obstruction can be bounded without claiming qualified outcomes.

No exhaustive goal categories, global change-direction enum, quantity, budget, achievement horizon or measurable success criterion are universally required. A compound business use can be declared coherently; unsupported or mutually inconsistent decisive parts remain unusable for the corresponding question rather than being forced into one category. Purpose describes **why**, configuration describes **how currently configured**, and N06 reasons about their relation.

## 8. Object-scope semantics

Reuse the existing physical Business/Workspace identity. A purpose assertion is business-scoped and must reference an exact native directed-effort object through its provider, account/object namespace, native identity and the evidenced business/source linkage. Preserve native kind when needed to disambiguate identity: campaign, ad set, ad group, line item or another native object. Names and display labels never identify the object reliably.

The existing registered source/operation scope provides acquisition/access provenance, not the business purpose. Retain the source references through which object identity/linkage was established without declaring different observations or source paths different purposes. Conversely, duplicate names/IDs across providers/accounts or unproven reconnect mappings must not merge objects. A new acquisition path requires an established identity-continuity relation before reusing the purpose reference.

Provider owners retain hierarchy, native identity lifecycle and parent membership. No universal taxonomy, cross-provider object equivalence or universal hierarchy engine is introduced. If native IDs can be reused, the identity owner must establish the same object/incarnation for the applicable time; ambiguity makes applicability unknown. Purpose versions do not repair missing identity proof.

One declaration may explicitly cover a finite identified object set for one business. Membership belongs to that declaration's version; adding/removing a member is material. “All future campaigns,” an account-wide fallback, a name pattern or an open descendant selector is outside this bounded contract. Declaring several objects together neither merges their identities nor proves comparable configuration.

## 9. Applicability and inheritance semantics

**Selected minimum: direct or explicitly enumerated applicability; no automatic inheritance.** A declaration for a parent applies to the parent only unless the authorized confirmed declaration explicitly includes identified children. Parent membership alone does not attest that every child's business use matches it.

When a business says the parent purpose also applies to specified descendants, record that as explicit coverage in the accepted purpose revision. If the claim relies on descent, preserve the exact parent/child membership evidence and applicable time through the native identity responsibility, plus the declarer's confirmation of purpose applicability. Native membership evidence proves descent; business confirmation proves business-use applicability. Neither substitutes for the other. A newly created child or a changed parent relation has no automatic coverage.

An exception is an explicit authorized scope change, not “child always wins.” For parent P and children C1/C2, a confirmed revision can cover P/C1 and explicitly exclude C2 from that declaration, with a separate accepted direct purpose for C2. Preserve the superseded coverage, new coverage, exception and exact versions. Excluding C2 without a replacement leaves its purpose unknown; it does not assert no purpose.

If an existing broad declaration still explicitly covers C2 and an independent child declaration states an incompatible use for the same time, preserve the overlap as conflict until an authorized correction explicitly resolves coverage/content. Neither timestamp, native depth nor declarer rank silently selects the winner. Explicit authorized replacement of an identified prior declaration is a transition, not a latest-wins rule.

If descent, identity, coverage, exception or time cannot be established, **applicability is UNKNOWN**, and N06 cannot use that parent purpose for the child. This supports a parent purpose with a child exception without an inheritance engine. Unbounded/transitive implicit inheritance remains unsupported; future support would require separate accepted semantics.

## 10. DTM relationship

DTM remains N02's optional business-wide set of desired customer-origin countries, with exact Context revision and neutral country identities. A purpose may explicitly state that particular effort serves a part of that set. “Acquire customers from Saudi Arabia” can legitimately specialize DTM `{Saudi Arabia, UAE}`; it does not rewrite the set or require every object to cover both countries.

Where the declared relation is “serve this subset of DTM,” preserve the exact Context/DTM revision and explicit subset relation. A later DTM revision does not silently change that relation. A literal declared Saudi customer purpose can remain known after DTM changes, while its claimed alignment with **current** DTM needs reassessment. If the purpose specifically depends on the old Context rather than having standalone meaning, withhold current dependency-sensitive use until applicability is confirmed.

A declaration outside DTM is not automatically invalid: DTM is not an exclusive prohibition and experimental/business reasons may exist. Record the actual relation or unresolved tension; N08 owns broader coherence. Do not invent missing commercial/customer restrictions or use purpose as a second DTM writer. Country identity equality does not establish the configuration/customer-origin bridge required by Discovery E4.

## 11. Current Intent relationship

N03 remains optional and **BUSINESS_WIDE_ONLY**. Object purpose may exist independently when no Intent is declared, reference an exact confirmed Intent revision when the business explicitly relates it, or describe a supporting specialization without changing global Intent. No compulsory reference or cloned global Intent bundle.

Preserve whether the relation is an explicit declarer assertion, a required dependency of the purpose, or no relation established. “Supports sales Intent revision I1 through awareness effort” is an asserted relationship; it does not prove causal effectiveness. Awareness effort alongside global sales intent is not automatically incompatible. Difference in level, method or scope is not contradiction.

A genuine tension can remain between independently legitimate declarations. Purpose acceptance does not validate global coherence or invalidate N03. N06 retains material qualifications and withholds conclusions needing an unresolved alignment premise; N08 later reconciles relevant cross-domain coherence without back-writing either declaration. No cross-domain reconciliation rules are designed here.

A later Intent change does not erase the purpose or mutate its historical reference. Standalone use may still apply; dependent use needs revalidation against the changed Intent. N03's quantity, deadline and outcome acceptance are not copied, inferred or overridden. Purpose has no right to mutate `business_context_version` semantics through this document.

## 12. Provider-native objective/configuration relationship

Provider objective, optimization goal, bid strategy, targeting and delivery settings remain source-native facts faithfully represented through N04/N12 and qualified where relevant by N05. They are not canonical object purpose. A provider “Traffic” objective can coexist with a business declaration “generate property inquiries”; no equivalence or contradiction follows from labels alone.

Even apparent agreement needs explicit established meaning before it supports a comparison. An advertiser may mention a native setting as supporting evidence, but must explicitly confirm the business purpose independently. A source configuration revision does not change purpose. A purpose revision does not change provider configuration. Known targeting and unknown purpose may coexist honestly.

No provider API research, new native mapping, configuration admission rule or inference from names, performance, sector, account ownership, DTM or Intent is performed or authorized.

## 14. Revision, currentness and history

A purpose identity distinguishes this business-scoped declaration through its lifetime; each material accepted transition has an exact immutable revision identity and retrievable full meaning. This is a semantic identity requirement, not an ID format or entity. Different simultaneous object uses may have different declaration identities; one identity can cover an explicit set. No duplicate editable “current truth” inside domains.

New revision required for changed business use, decisive intended customer relation, covered objects/exception, business-effective applicability, dependent Context/Intent relation, withdrawal, or a material correction/authority re-attestation changing what is accepted. A repeat with unchanged meaning/applicability under unchanged basis need not invent a new substantive purpose; any materially new attestation/qualification basis consumed is separately identifiable. Translation or spelling that preserves meaning cannot silently change it.

Replacement must explicitly identify the declaration/version it supersedes and its effective boundary. Concurrent incompatible statements do not replace each other merely by arriving later. Withdrawal ends the accepted current declaration for its scope, returning unsupported future purpose use to unknown rather than asserting the object has no purpose. Reuse of the same object for a new purpose is a new revision/linked replacement, never overwrite.

**Current means applicable to the specified business/object and business-effective time, under the accepted declaration's continuity/limits and present knowledge, with no known superseding/withdrawal transition or unresolved decisive challenge.** It does not mean newest row or youngest artifact. Distinguish “known declared purpose” from “sufficiently supported purpose now.” Preserve unknown validity when continuity cannot be established; no TTL or periodic compulsory reconfirmation is invented.

An authorized declaration normally takes effect on acceptance unless a distinct effective time/interval is explicitly asserted and supported. A historical attestation recorded later must preserve both claimed effective time and later acceptance/knowledge time; it cannot pretend Builderad knew it earlier. No evidence means no backdated-current claim. Future-effective assertions cannot be consumed early. This does not widen N03's existing no-backdating/time rules.

An N06 conclusion retains the exact purpose version/full retrievable meaning, applicability basis, effective time and knowledge/qualification basis it actually consumed. Mid-period changes require partitioned supported reasoning or withholding across the unresolved interval, not assigning today's purpose to the whole month. Later corrections produce new purpose/conclusion/decision versions when warranted. An earlier decision remains interpretable as **what it consumed then**, even if later shown mistaken; correction never manufactures earlier knowledge.

Loss of current authority to a source does not rewrite what was historically declared or grant ongoing access. If retrieval is restricted, expose the limitation to authorized consumers. Revocation/correction challenging the original declaration requires explicit qualification/correction, not automatic deletion of the old accepted fact.

## 15. Unknown and conflict semantics

**UNKNOWN != NONE; UNAUTHORIZED != UNKNOWN FACT; CONFLICTING != LATEST WINS; STALE != FALSE; MISSING != INFERRED PURPOSE.** This minimum contract does not establish an explicit “object has no purpose” declaration category. Clear/absence cannot create it. A statement “no current business use is assigned” would need precise separately admitted meaning before a consumer treats it as a positive assertion, and still would not prove no delivery or authorize shutdown.

Genuine declaration conflict requires materially incompatible assertions about the same business use/decisive premise, overlapping exact object applicability and effective time, with no established replacement/exception relation. Unknown equivalence cannot prove conflict. Acquisition and awareness purposes, or narrower DTM coverage, can coexist; an ambiguous combination is not silently resolved. Compatibility with global Context/Intent is a distinct N08 coherence matter, not a timestamp-based admission rule.

## 17. N06 consumption contract

N06 may consume a purpose premise only when the following meaning is available and legitimate for the actual question:

1. Exact business and **accepted purpose identity/version**, with the full confirmed business-use premise and decisive qualifications retrievable.
2. Exact object applicability: direct/finite covered membership, exclusions and any relied-on native relation evidence; no guessed inheritance.
3. Business-effective time/interval, evaluation knowledge time, currentness and known supersession/correction/continuity limits.
4. Attributable declaration/provenance, established business declaration authority/acceptance and current protected-read authority, with distinct qualification state.
5. Exact Context/DTM and optional Intent revision relationships **where actually relied on**, distinguishing literal standalone meaning from dependent applicability.

N06 selects an explicitly established relevant premise; it cannot create, rewrite or reinterpret purpose, infer it from configuration/name/performance, silently admit raw prose, resolve authority conflicts or promote ambiguous/unaccepted statements to facts. A canonical declaration is necessary but not automatically sufficient for a targeting implication. Unrelated unsupported purpose detail must not become a universal gate for independent questions.

For the first customer-country proposition, N06 additionally needs E1's exact DTM, E3's effective configuration, E4's justified native eligibility/customer-origin relationship and E5's admissibility/currentness. Known purpose plus a different configured-country label alone proves no obstruction. Subset targeting may serve a legitimate subset purpose. An obstruction conclusion asserts only the supported material directed-effort implication; it proves no missed sale, waste, optimal allocation or instruction to execute.

Historical retained input copies are records of use, not additional mutable canonical owners.

N08 owns broader coherent decisions; N07 retains resource reasoning. Purpose alone authorizes neither resource commitment nor action.
